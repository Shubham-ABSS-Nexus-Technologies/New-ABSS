Meet the Founder

Shubham Kumar is a BCA student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, with a strong interest in web development, digital products and practical technology solutions.

He founded ABSS Nexus Technologies with the goal of helping businesses and individuals create professional, accessible and user-friendly digital experiences.
